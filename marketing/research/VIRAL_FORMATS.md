# Viral short-video formats in the trading niche (vidIQ outlier scan)

**Source:** vidIQ Instagram + TikTok outlier search. Query: "memecoin trading humor: getting rugged, snipers and bundlers, dev sold, crypto trenches skits and AI cinematic parody". Audience: global crypto Twitter/TikTok, 18–35. Window: last 30 days.

**Results:** 16 posts that ran 4×–370× above their creator's median views (575K–4.6M views).

**Caveat:** this scan covers Instagram and TikTok, not X. The formats travel because X's video player autoplays muted, loops short clips, and rewards completion and replies. Treat the patterns as hypotheses to test, not guarantees.

## Production rules that follow

- **The hook is the first frame.** Big text that makes sense with the sound off, plus motion within 0.5 s. No logo intro.
- **Short and loopable.** 6–15 s for memes, 20–35 s for explainers. End on a frame that cuts cleanly back to frame 0.
- **A hit on every cut:** a stinger, whoosh or low boom. Music-only mixes need a drop in the first 2 s.
- **Burned-in captions** on every voiceover, because X autoplays muted.
- **No profit claims or "copy this trader for profit" hooks.** That's the one pattern from the scan we do NOT copy, because the ledger forbids it.
